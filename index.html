WHY IT MATTERS

Nature Needs Our Protection

Wildlife conservation protects animals, plants, and the environments they depend on. Habitat destruction, pollution, climate change, and illegal hunting threaten biodiversity. Responsible choices and collective action can help protect our natural world for generations to come.
